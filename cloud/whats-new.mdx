What each production release of Lasso Cloud adds, with a link to the page that
explains it. To check what's serving right now, read `source_sha` from
[`/api/health`](https://lasso.sh/api/health). RPC Core releases have their own
notes on [GitHub](https://github.com/jaxernst/lasso-rpc/releases).

<Update label="November 14, 2026" description="Release 312" tags={["Custom profiles", "Dashboard", "Pricing", "Accounts"]}>

- **Test a Custom endpoint as soon as it's live.** After you activate a Custom
  profile, its overview lists each chain's endpoint with a copy button and
  **Send test request**. One click sends `eth_blockNumber` from your browser
  and shows the round-trip time and the provider that answered.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles)
- **One Custom payment activates every waiting profile.** Starting Checkout
  from a profile or from Subscribe opens the same Custom subscription. Once
  it's paid, every profile waiting on it activates, up to your plan's profile
  limit. A profile past the limit stays pending, and the agent API answers
  `409 profile_limit`.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#limits)
- **See a month's price before you start.** The pricing page and `llms.txt`
  work through one month of Premium: 2M `eth_call` requests on load balanced
  use 6M CU, which costs $30 from a balance. `llms.txt` also opens its funding
  step with the no-account path: `POST /api/v1/credit` returns a funded key
  for $0.05 or more, paid with any x402 or MPP client.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)
- **Unused provisional accounts close after 24 hours.** A provisional account
  that nothing has used closes 24 hours after it was created. Sending a
  request, funding it, saving a Custom profile or holding an unexpired claim
  link keeps it open. A closed account's key answers `401 account_closed`;
  create a new one with `POST /api/v1/keys`.
  [Accounts and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents#limits)

</Update>

<Update label="November 12, 2026, second release" description="Release 311" tags={["Custom profiles", "Dashboard", "Billing", "Reliability"]}>

- **Seven free Custom days, no card.** An account that has never had Custom
  gets seven free days the first time it activates a profile, from the
  dashboard or with `PUT /api/v1/profiles/{slug}`. There's no Checkout step.
  The dashboard shows when the trial ends, and the agent API returns
  `trial_ends_at`. Subscribe from the trial pill, the profile panel or the
  account page.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#limits)
- **A lapsed Custom profile says so.** When a trial, grant or ended
  subscription runs out, requests to that profile's URL return HTTP 402
  `custom_access_expired`, with a `next_action` that says how to buy Custom.
  Before, they were served and charged as `premium`. Your profile and
  its configuration are kept, and buying Custom puts it back on your
  providers. A subscription whose payment is past due still serves as
  `premium`, marked `x-lasso-fallback: profile`, while Stripe retries.
  [Keys and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles#how-a-profile-segment-resolves)
- **Checkout works for every new account.** Subscribing to Custom from the
  dashboard no longer fails with "Stripe rejected the request" for accounts
  created at sign-up.
- **Trial links work right after sign-up.** A person who signs up from a Custom
  trial link can accept it straight away, without visiting the dashboard
  first.
- **The editor names a provider's credential conflict.** A provider whose URL
  carries a username and password and also has an `authorization` header or
  API key now shows that conflict, instead of a passing check.
- **Streams reads wait through a cold start.** A Streams read with `wait` that
  lands on a feed that hasn't loaded yet now waits for it, instead of
  answering `503 cold_start`.
- **Steadier failover.** A log subscription keeps its replacement provider
  eligible to fill a gap during failover, and HTTP failover still works after a
  stalled WebSocket. Lasso also recognizes Arbitrum's `eth_getLogs` range
  refusals.

</Update>

<Update label="November 12, 2026" description="Release 310" tags={["Custom profiles", "Dashboard", "Reliability"]}>

- **Add providers from one box.** On the dashboard canvas, paste provider URLs
  into a single add box. Lasso checks each one and tells you why it refused a
  provider, instead of reporting every refusal as a timeout. Custom setup also
  offers Ankr and Tenderly keys, and shows each vendor's logo.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#onboarding-what-lasso-checks)
- **Paid Custom requests stay on your providers.** After you pay for Custom
  through Checkout, Custom WebSocket requests are served by your own providers
  instead of Lasso's Public providers, and Custom responses no longer carry
  `x-lasso-fallback: payment`.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors#response-headers)
- **Provider URLs with credentials work.** A provider URL that carries a
  username and password (`https://user:pass@host/...`) now authenticates on
  both HTTP and WebSocket.
- **Shared providers say so.** Shared providers are labeled "Public shared" on
  the dashboard, and their rows show the block poll interval Lasso gives them.
- **A provider's billing problem isn't your error.** When a provider refuses a
  request because its own credits or quota ran out, Lasso tries another
  provider. Before, wording like "insufficient funds" could come back to you as
  a revert, or as a sender balance error on `eth_sendRawTransaction`.
- **Smarter retries after a refusal.** When a provider says it can't serve a
  method or an archive read, Lasso tries endpoints from other companies before
  other endpoints of the same company.
- **One error shape across the management API.** Unknown `/api/v1` paths now
  answer with the same `error` object as every other management error, and
  each error's `next_action` matches the operation you tried.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors#management-api-errors)

</Update>

<Update label="November 10, 2026, second release" description="Release 309" tags={["Streams", "Dashboard", "Reliability"]}>

- **Dense log blocks no longer close your subscriptions.** Lasso now admits
  WebSocket messages by size rather than by count, so a block with hundreds of
  matching logs reaches your `eth_subscribe` stream intact instead of ending
  it. [WebSocket continuity](/cloud/websocket-continuity-evidence)
- **Your key stays off the screen.** Endpoint URLs on the dashboard show the
  key as its prefix and an ellipsis. The Copy buttons still copy the full,
  working URL.
- **Accurate request rates on busy chains.** Dashboard request rates and
  provider shares now count every request instead of a sample, so they stay
  correct at high request rates.
- **A fallback always says so.** When a fallback serves your request on another
  profile, the response carries `x-lasso-fallback-profile` whether or not you
  asked for metadata.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors)
- **Adding a provider checks more.** When you add a provider on the dashboard,
  its check now reports whether it returns block data and whether its WebSocket
  URL accepts an upgrade.

</Update>

<Update label="November 10, 2026" description="Release 308" tags={["Custom profiles", "Dashboard"]}>

- **Turn a provider off without removing it.** In a Custom profile, set a
  provider's `enabled` to `false`, or switch it off on the dashboard. Lasso
  stops routing to it and stops its background checks, and keeps its URL,
  headers and settings so you can turn it back on later.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#the-document)
- **See when a change is waiting on a provider.** If an applied change would
  leave a chain without a reachable provider, Lasso keeps serving the previous
  version until the new provider's address resolves. The dashboard now shows
  that the change is waiting, and which routes can't connect yet and why.

</Update>

<Update label="November 4, 2026" description="Release 300" tags={["Transactions", "Routing", "Agents", "Custom profiles"]}>

- **Choose which providers receive signed transactions.** Set
  `transactions: {providers, fallback}` on a Custom profile chain to send signed
  transactions only to those providers, while reads and subscriptions use the
  others. `fallback: read_providers` adds a second send phase over the profile's
  other providers. Private, bundle, cancellation, conditional and synchronous
  send methods stay inside the same set. Stored profiles moved over automatically;
  replace the earlier `relays` and `public_fallback` names in any saved requests.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#the-document)
- **Methods are handled by what they do.** An unknown method carrying signed
  transaction bytes follows your send policy. Other unknown methods get one
  dispatch; when a provider refuses one with `-32601`, Lasso learns that and moves
  later requests to another provider. `lasso_meta` reports `execution_safety` and
  `classified_by`. [RPC method behavior](/cloud/rpc-behavior#standards-and-provider-differences)
- **Key responses return URL templates.** `rpc_url_template` and
  `ws_url_template` replace `rpc_url` and `ws_url`, and contain `{chain}`, so what
  you copy always says it needs a chain. Update code that reads the old fields;
  existing keys and URLs keep working. [Keys and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles)
- **New accounts come with their claim link.** Creating an account also returns
  `claim_url` and `claim_expires_at`, so an agent can hand it to a person without
  a second request. [Accounts and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **Paid Custom profiles go live on the first sign of payment.** A paid draft now
  activates as soon as any subscription event shows it paid, rather than waiting
  for one specific billing event. [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles)

</Update>

<Update label="October 15, 2026" description="Release 273" tags={["Agents", "Payments", "Custom profiles", "Usage"]}>

- **Buy a funded RPC key at one fixed URL.** `POST /api/v1/credit` creates an
  account and its first key through a standard x402 or MPP payment exchange,
  with no signup or account ID. Replaying the same signed payment recovers
  its credentials without crediting twice. [Funded keys](/cloud/balance-and-fallback#buy-a-funded-key)
- **Signed transactions fail over.** Lasso resends the same signed bytes to up
  to three providers in turn, stopping at a sender fault. Before returning an
  error, it looks the transaction up by hash and returns the hash if the
  transaction is pending or mined. [Transaction behavior](/cloud/rpc-behavior#transactions)
- **Read Lasso as Markdown.** Agents can request the homepage with
  `Accept: text/markdown`; discovery also includes structured application data
  and purchase price ranges and protocols.
  [Agent entry points](/cloud/reference/agent-entry-points)
- **Custom routing uses learned historical depth and method refusals.**
  Lasso moves providers known to be unsuitable behind alternatives, while
  keeping the last route available. Inspect learned evidence by node and
  transport, reset it, or suspend an individual hint for an hour.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#lasso-learns-your-providers)
- **See 30 days of usage by key and profile.** The Account page charts requests
  and CU, shows each key's share of traffic and per-profile breakdown, and
  brings included usage and balance spend together. Custom traffic is included.
  [Routing evidence](/cloud/observability)

</Update>

<Update label="October 12, 2026" description="Release 267" tags={["Agents", "Accounts", "Payments", "Pricing"]}>

- **Agents fund an account with the RPC key alone.** The key reads its account
  at `GET /api/v1/accounts/current`, and a top-up credits that account's `id`.
  The management token is only needed to add keys, rotate them, build Custom
  profiles or hand the account over. [Quickstart](/cloud/quickstart)
- **Top-ups settle the moment you check them.** Reading a pending purchase's
  `status_url` settles it from the chain.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **One account guide and a published error catalog.** `/SKILL.md` and
  `/llms.txt` describe the account API only, and `openapi.json` publishes every
  error Lasso originates, with its fix, in `x-error-catalog`.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors)
- **The claim page shows exactly what moves**: the keys, the combined balance
  when you add it to an account you have, and that a merge can't be undone.
  The account menu switches between your accounts.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **A new pricing page** shows Free, Pro and Custom side by side, read from the
  live price sheet. [Pricing](https://lasso.sh/pricing)
- **Pro includes 10,000,000 CU a month** from each subscription's next period.
  A current period keeps its allowance until it renews.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

### Changes to note

- **Premium URLs without a strategy now use `balanced-fast`**, priced at 1.5×
  instead of `load-balanced`'s 1×. Name `load-balanced` in the URL to keep the
  previous routing and price. [Routing](/cloud/routing)
- **`latency-weighted` is now `balanced-fast`.** The old name keeps working.
- **The URL selects the profile.** `rpc_url` and `ws_url` end in
  `/profile/premium`; replace that segment to use another profile. Keys no
  longer have a default profile, and sending `default_profile` returns 422
  `unknown_field`. [Keys, URLs and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles)

</Update>

<Update label="October 3–8, 2026" description="Releases 258–265: accounts for people and agents" tags={["Accounts", "Payments", "API"]}>

Lasso Cloud moved to accounts on October 7. Your existing RPC URLs and keys kept
working through the change.

### Accounts for people and agents

- **One account holds your keys, profiles and balance.** People and agents work
  on it as members, each with their own credential and permissions, and the
  dashboard shows its balance, subscriptions and usage per key.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **An agent can set up Lasso without a signup.** One unauthenticated
  `POST /api/v1/keys` creates an account with its agent, a first key and a
  $0.025 starting balance, and returns its RPC URL.
  [Quickstart](/cloud/quickstart)
- **Hand an agent's setup to a person.** The agent requests a single-use claim
  link. Claiming keeps it as a new account or merges it into one you already
  have, with a preview of the combined balance, keys, rate limits and agents
  before you confirm. Every key and URL keeps working.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **Bring an agent into an existing account.** The agent asks to connect and
  gets an approval link; once an admin approves, it works on that account.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **Rotate a key with no downtime.** The old secret keeps serving for the grace
  period you choose, so each deploy switches over in its own time.
  [Rotate a key](/cloud/guides/rotate-a-key)

### One balance, priced per method

- **One USD balance per account.** Every request is priced by method and
  strategy, and each response tells you what it cost in `x-lasso-cu` and
  `x-lasso-usd`. [Chains and prices](/cloud/reference/chains-and-prices)
- **Top up with a stablecoin wallet, no Lasso credential needed.** Credit
  requests answer with a standard 402 challenge for x402 (USDC on Base) or MPP
  (USDC.e on Tempo); any stock client can pay it.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **Your app keeps serving when the money runs out.** An account that has paid
  before falls back to public providers for 30 days while you top up, marked
  with `x-lasso-fallback: payment`.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **Free access, Pro and Custom are account subscriptions.** Included usage is
  counted in CU per month, and usage beyond it draws on the balance.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

### API and signals

- **The account API lives at `/api/v1`**, described in full by
  [`openapi.json`](https://lasso.sh/openapi.json). Agent API v2 RPC URLs keep
  working.
- **WebSocket RPC on every key**, including keys an agent creates.
  [Keys and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles)
- **Every response explains itself.** `x-lasso-request-id`, `x-lasso-access`,
  `x-lasso-balance-usd` and the `RateLimit` headers show who served the request,
  what it cost and how much room is left. Errors that Lasso originates carry a
  `code` and a `next_action`, listed in one reference.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors)

### Changes to note

- An RPC key can no longer request a claim link for an account an agent
  created; the agent requests it. Accounts migrated from keys that predate
  agents still claim with their key.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- Keys an agent creates share their account's rate limit instead of each
  getting 50 RPS. [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing#rate-limits)
- Free access is counted in per-method CU instead of bytes.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

</Update>
