What each production release of Lasso Cloud adds, with a link to the page that
explains it. To check what's serving right now, read `source_sha` from
[`/api/health`](https://lasso.sh/api/health). RPC Core releases have their own
notes on [GitHub](https://github.com/jaxernst/lasso-rpc/releases).

<Update label="October 15, 2026" description="Release 273" tags={["Agents", "Payments", "Custom profiles", "Usage"]}>

- **Buy a funded RPC key at one fixed URL.** `POST /api/v1/credit` creates an
  account and its first key through a standard x402 or MPP payment exchange,
  with no signup or account ID. Replaying the same signed payment recovers
  its credentials without crediting twice. [Funded keys](/cloud/balance-and-fallback#buy-a-funded-key)
- **Signed transactions fail over.** Lasso resends the same signed bytes to up
  to three providers in turn, stopping at a sender fault. Before returning an
  error, it looks the transaction up by hash and returns the hash if the
  transaction is pending or mined. [Transaction behavior](/cloud/rpc-behavior#transactions)
- **Read Lasso as Markdown.** Agents can request the homepage with
  `Accept: text/markdown`; discovery also includes structured application data
  and purchase price ranges and protocols.
  [Agent entry points](/cloud/reference/agent-entry-points)
- **Custom routing uses learned historical depth and method refusals.**
  Lasso moves providers known to be unsuitable behind alternatives, while
  keeping the last route available. Inspect learned evidence by node and
  transport, reset it, or suspend an individual hint for an hour.
  [Custom profiles](/cloud/custom-profiles#lasso-learns-your-providers)
- **See 30 days of usage by key and profile.** The Account page charts requests
  and CU, shows each key's share of traffic and per-profile breakdown, and
  brings included usage and balance spend together. Custom traffic is included.
  [Routing evidence](/cloud/observability)

</Update>

<Update label="October 12, 2026" description="Release 267" tags={["Agents", "Accounts", "Payments", "Pricing"]}>

- **Agents fund an account with the RPC key alone.** The key reads its account
  at `GET /api/v1/accounts/current`, and a top-up credits that account's `id`.
  The management token is only needed to add keys, rotate them, build Custom
  profiles or hand the account over. [Quickstart](/cloud/quickstart)
- **Top-ups settle the moment you check them.** Reading a pending purchase's
  `status_url` settles it from the chain.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **One account guide and a published error catalog.** `/SKILL.md` and
  `/llms.txt` describe the account API only, and `openapi.json` publishes every
  error Lasso originates, with its fix, in `x-error-catalog`.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors)
- **The claim page shows exactly what moves**: the keys, the combined balance
  when you add it to an account you have, and that a merge can't be undone.
  The account menu switches between your accounts.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **A new pricing page** shows Free, Pro and Custom side by side, read from the
  live price sheet. [Pricing](https://lasso.sh/pricing)
- **Pro includes 10,000,000 CU a month** from each subscription's next period.
  A current period keeps its allowance until it renews.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

### Changes to note

- **Premium URLs without a strategy now use `balanced-fast`**, priced at 1.5×
  instead of `load-balanced`'s 1×. Name `load-balanced` in the URL to keep the
  previous routing and price. [Routing](/cloud/routing)
- **`latency-weighted` is now `balanced-fast`.** The old name keeps working.
- **The URL selects the profile.** `rpc_url` and `ws_url` end in
  `/profile/premium`; replace that segment to use another profile. Keys no
  longer have a default profile, and sending `default_profile` returns 422
  `unknown_field`. [Keys, URLs and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles)

</Update>

<Update label="October 9, 2026" description="Release 266" tags={["Payments", "Custom profiles"]}>

- **Buy Custom access with a stablecoin wallet.** A single purchase can now be
  up to \$100, enough for up to 30 days of Custom access at $1.30 a day, with no
  subscription or signup. An agent can put your own providers behind Lasso and
  pay for the trial itself. [Trial your own providers](/cloud/bring-your-own-rpc)

</Update>

<Update label="October 3–8, 2026" description="Releases 258–265: accounts for people and agents" tags={["Accounts", "Payments", "API"]}>

Lasso Cloud moved to accounts on October 7. Your existing RPC URLs and keys kept
working through the change.

### Accounts for people and agents

- **One account holds your keys, profiles and balance.** People and agents work
  on it as members, each with their own credential and permissions, and the
  dashboard shows its balance, subscriptions and usage per key.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **An agent can set up Lasso without a signup.** One unauthenticated
  `POST /api/v1/keys` creates an account with its agent, a first key and a
  $0.025 starting balance, and returns its RPC URL.
  [Quickstart](/cloud/quickstart)
- **Hand an agent's setup to a person.** The agent requests a single-use claim
  link. Claiming keeps it as a new account or merges it into one you already
  have, with a preview of the combined balance, keys, rate limits and agents
  before you confirm. Every key and URL keeps working.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **Bring an agent into an existing account.** The agent asks to connect and
  gets an approval link; once an admin approves, it works on that account.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- **Rotate a key with no downtime.** The old secret keeps serving for the grace
  period you choose, so each deploy switches over in its own time.
  [Rotate a key](/cloud/guides/rotate-a-key)

### One balance, priced per method

- **One USD balance per account.** Every request is priced by method and
  strategy, and each response tells you what it cost in `x-lasso-cu` and
  `x-lasso-usd`. [Chains and prices](/cloud/reference/chains-and-prices)
- **Top up with a stablecoin wallet, no Lasso credential needed.** Credit
  requests answer with a standard 402 challenge for x402 (USDC on Base) or MPP
  (USDC.e on Tempo); any stock client can pay it.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **Your app keeps serving when the money runs out.** An account that has paid
  before falls back to public providers for 30 days while you top up, marked
  with `x-lasso-fallback: payment`.
  [Balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)
- **Free access, Pro and Custom are account subscriptions.** Included usage is
  counted in CU per month, and usage beyond it draws on the balance.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

### API and signals

- **The account API lives at `/api/v1`**, described in full by
  [`openapi.json`](https://lasso.sh/openapi.json). Agent API v2 RPC URLs keep
  working.
- **WebSocket RPC on every key**, including keys an agent creates.
  [Keys and profiles](/cloud/keys-and-profiles)
- **Every response explains itself.** `x-lasso-request-id`, `x-lasso-access`,
  `x-lasso-balance-usd` and the `RateLimit` headers show who served the request,
  what it cost and how much room is left. Errors that Lasso originates carry a
  `code` and a `next_action`, listed in one reference.
  [Signals and errors](/cloud/reference/signals-and-errors)

### Changes to note

- An RPC key can no longer request a claim link for an account an agent
  created; the agent requests it. Accounts migrated from keys that predate
  agents still claim with their key.
  [Accounts, people and agents](/cloud/accounts-and-agents)
- Keys an agent creates share their account's rate limit instead of each
  getting 50 RPS. [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing#rate-limits)
- Free access is counted in per-method CU instead of bytes.
  [Serving access and pricing](/cloud/serving-access-and-pricing)

</Update>
