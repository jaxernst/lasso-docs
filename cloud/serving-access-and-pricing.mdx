# Serving access and pricing

Every request Lasso serves has one **serving access**: `premium`, `public` or
`custom`. It decides which providers answer, what the request costs, which
strategies apply and which rate limit counts it, and every response tells you
which one served it. Read this page to choose a pool and predict your bill;
read [balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback) for what
happens when money runs out.

```bash
curl -sS -D - -o /dev/null "$LASSO_RPC_URL/fastest/base" \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_blockNumber","params":[]}' \
  | grep -iE '^x-lasso-(access|usd|cu|fallback)'
```

```http
x-lasso-access: premium
x-lasso-cu: 2
x-lasso-usd: 0.000010
```

## The three kinds of serving access

| Serving access | Providers | Strategies | Price | Rate limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `premium` | Lasso's premium pool | All four | Charged per request | 100 RPS |
| `public` | Lasso's public pool | `load-balanced` only | Uncharged | 30 RPS |
| `custom` | Your Custom profile's providers | All four | Uncharged; your Custom subscription covers it | 1,000 RPS per profile |

Your URL names the profile, and your account's payment state decides how it is
served:

| The URL names | Served as |
|---|---|
| `premium`, or no profile with a `premium` default | `premium` while included usage or balance covers it; otherwise `public`, marked with `x-lasso-fallback` (see [balance and payment fallback](/cloud/balance-and-fallback)) |
| `public` | `public`, always on public providers and uncharged, while the account's balance or included usage, grace window or free allowance would serve it; otherwise 402 `balance_exhausted`. It counts against the free allowance only when free access is what serves it |
| A Custom profile | `custom` while your account has Custom access; otherwise `premium`, marked `x-lasso-fallback: profile` |

Choose `public` explicitly for traffic that doesn't need premium providers:
background jobs, development, or a browser app. Same key, different path
segment:

```ts
const premium = `${process.env.LASSO_RPC_URL}/fastest/base`;
const background = `${process.env.LASSO_RPC_URL}/profile/public/base`;
```

## What a premium request costs

A request's size in **compute units (CU)** is its **method weight** times its
**strategy factor**. Its price is that CU at a published USD rate. A cheap read
like `eth_blockNumber` weighs less than `eth_getLogs`, and a trace weighs
more. The strategy factor is the trade you make between latency and cost:

| Strategy | Factor |
|---|---|
| `load-balanced` | 1x |
| `latency-weighted` | 1.5x |
| `fastest` | 2x |
| `priority` | 1x |

Every method's weight and price is in
[chains and prices](/cloud/reference/chains-and-prices), generated from the
live [`agent.json`](https://lasso.sh/agent.json). Every response states its
size in `x-lasso-cu` and its charge in `x-lasso-usd`, so nothing is a surprise.
A batch is priced per item, a WebSocket request costs the same as over HTTP,
and each subscription notification is priced as an `eth_subscription` entry.

Money is always in USD: prices, balances, purchases and charges. Included
usage, from free access or a subscription, is counted in CU per month.

## Subscriptions and free access

| | What it gives your account | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Free access** | A monthly CU allowance served on public providers, uncharged | A person on the account sponsors it; each person sponsors up to three accounts |
| **Pro** | A monthly CU allowance on premium providers; beyond it, requests draw on the balance | Subscribe in the dashboard |
| **Custom** | Routing over your own providers on up to five Custom profiles, unmetered | Subscribe in the dashboard, or buy days with a wallet, even before anyone signs up |

Free access belongs to accounts that a person has joined. When you sign in
and create or claim your first account, you sponsor it, and you can sponsor
two more. A provisional account an agent created has no free access until a
person claims it.

Current subscription prices and allowances are on
[lasso.sh/pricing](https://lasso.sh/pricing) and in `agent.json`.

## Rate limits

Limits apply per account, not per key, and per place requests are served:

- One **managed bucket** holds every `premium` and `public` request from all
  of the account's keys.
- Each Custom profile has its own bucket while it serves `custom`.

Every response carries the IETF `RateLimit-Policy` and `RateLimit` headers,
named after the bucket (`managed` or the Custom profile's slug). Over the
limit, Lasso answers 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After`. Need more? Ask
Lasso support for an account override.

## Limits

- `public` serves `load-balanced` only. A request for another strategy is
  served as `load-balanced`, and `lasso_meta.strategy` reports what was used.
  The same holds for any request served by payment fallback or free access.
- Public providers' method coverage and historical depth can be narrower than
  the premium pool's.
- Rate limits are enforced on each serving machine.
- Custom traffic has no request price, but your upstream providers may charge
  you for it.
