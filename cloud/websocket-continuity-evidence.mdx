title: WebSocket continuity evidence
description: Scope and result of Lasso's controlled newHeads provider-replacement soak.
---

In a controlled 30-minute soak, a synthetic test rather than production
traffic, 50 clients sharing one `newHeads` subscription rode
through 119 forced provider replacements with zero gaps and zero duplicates.
The test covered one profile, one running process and `newHeads`; clients that
disconnect resubscribe from their own checkpoint (see
[WebSocket subscriptions](/advanced/websocket-subscriptions#application-checkpoints-and-reorgs)).

## How it ran

On August 22, 2026, Lasso forced a provider replacement every 15 seconds and
skipped three live blocks each time, so every replacement had to backfill the
gap over HTTP. One upstream subscription served all 50 clients throughout, and
its state drained after the last client left.

with Lasso Cloud's test harness, which isn't part of the public RPC Core
repository.
