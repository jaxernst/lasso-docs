# RPC routing for indexers and historical backfills

Indexers and backfills spread heavy historical reads across providers with
`load-balanced`, so one provider's quota stops being your ceiling. Historical
state, receipts and logs have different coverage across providers, so qualify
your exact queries through your exact Lasso URL before you move.

```ts
const backfill = `${process.env.LASSO_RPC_URL}/load-balanced/ethereum`;
```

## Qualify the queries you depend on

Build a small set of known-good answers from your own contracts and history:
an expected state value, a block hash, a nonempty log set or a receipt. Run
each through the direct provider and through Lasso.

| Indexer task | Trial query | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Rebuild state | `eth_call` or a state method at a known old block | The expected value, with both selector forms your client uses |
| Scan events | `eth_getLogs` over a representative range | Every known log is present; range and result-size limits |
| Reconcile transactions | `eth_getTransactionReceipt` for known hashes | Receipt identity and block |

An empty result doesn't prove a provider covered the history you asked for.

## Choose the pool

| Pool | When |
|---|---|
| Lasso's `premium` pool | You want commercial providers with deep history and no provider accounts of your own |
| A Custom profile with an archive-capable provider | You already pay for archive access or run an archive node |
| `public` | Only for recent blocks; public providers' history is shallower |

Lasso's [historical workload evidence](/cloud/historical-workloads) shows dated
results across chains, depths and methods. Use it as a model for your own
test, not as a coverage promise.

## Handle range limits

When a provider refuses an `eth_getLogs` range as too large, Lasso returns
`-32005` with `data.reason: "log_range_too_large"` and
`data.action: "reduce_block_range"`. Split the range and retry; Lasso doesn't
split it for you. On a Custom profile, Lasso learns each provider's span limit
from these refusals and shows it in the profile's knowledge.

## Limits

- Batches route per item. A batch isn't pinned to one provider or one block.
  For related reads at one block, use [read at one block](/advanced/read-at-one-block).
- Keep a durable checkpoint and a reorg overlap, and deduplicate logs by
  identity.
- A refused range can still be charged on premium; check `x-lasso-usd`.
- Test from your deployment regions under representative volume, and keep the
  previous endpoint until the Lasso route passes.
